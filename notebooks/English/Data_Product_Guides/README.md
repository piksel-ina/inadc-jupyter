# Data Product Guides

---

> **Status:** This module is under development.

The Data Product Guides will cover individual Earth observation products available through the Open Data Cube. They will build on the [Beginner's Guide](../Beginners_Guide/README.md) and focus on the characteristics and correct handling of each product.

## Planned guides

- Landsat surface reflectance
- Landsat surface temperature
- Sentinel-1 radar backscatter
- Sentinel-2 surface reflectance

## Reference notebooks

The following notebooks were authored by [Alex Leith](https://github.com/alexgleith). They are retained as references while this series is developed, and their examples will inform the notebooks created for the planned guides.

- [Sentinel-1 water analysis](01_Sentinel-1_Water.ipynb)
- [Landsat surface reflectance](03_Landsat_SR_GettingStarted.ipynb)
- [Landsat surface temperature](03_Landsat_ST_GettingStarted.ipynb)
- [Sentinel-2 surface reflectance](04_Sentinel-2_GettingStarted.ipynb)

The Sentinel-1 notebook is a water-analysis case study. It will serve as a reference for the planned Sentinel-1 radar backscatter guide.

Each guide is expected to cover:

- the product and its source collection;
- available measurements, spatial resolution, and units;
- scale factors, offsets, and no-data values;
- quality measurements and cloud masking;
- true-colour and false-colour visualisation;
- a small product-specific example.

These guides will provide the product knowledge needed for the later Analysis Guides.
